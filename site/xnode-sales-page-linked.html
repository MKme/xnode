Why XNODE exists

Phones and tablets are powerful, but they are not always the fastest tool in a moving operation. XNODE gives the operator a wearable tactical surface: check the map, see TOC alerts, send a routine position check-in, or trigger a high-priority SOS without digging out another device.

Device-ready common operating picture

XTOC and XCOM can install the active raster map onto the device and sync overlay markers for team members, mesh nodes, CHECKINs, SITREPs, CONTACTs, TASKs, assets, routes, zones, and more.

Two-tap field reporting

The firmware can transmit clear XTOC packet text over the Meshtastic radio for Manual SOS and CheckIn flows, using the configured roster Unit ID and current device location.

Local-first by design

Maps and overlay data live on the device. Synced overlays persist in watch flash and reload when the tactical map opens or the watch reboots.

Real watch screens, not vaporware.

The current firmware builds for the T-Watch S3, T-Watch Ultra, T-Deck Plus, and T-Deck Pro. Across the supported targets, XNODE exposes its BLE bridge, installs a compact basemap, displays operational overlays, and keeps alerts visible on the device.

Supported T-Deck hardware

T-Deck support is built and tested as part of the same repository as the active watch firmware. Each handheld has its own board definition, hardware path, and Launcher-ready firmware output.

T-Deck Plus

  • 320x240 LCD interface with touch and a physical I2C keyboard.
  • GPS defaults on for map position and GPS time sync, with readable receiver diagnostics.
  • Onboard SX1262 LoRa, SD, battery monitoring, and the shared BLE/XTOC/XCOM workflow.
  • Display timeout keeps touch, keyboard, and LVGL responsive while GPS remains available.
  • Built and flashed on attached hardware; broader field validation is still ongoing.

T-Deck Pro

  • Portrait 240x320 e-paper interface with a dedicated local Pro hardware layer.
  • HYN touch, tap and swipe navigation, plus TCA8418 physical keyboard input.
  • DRV2605 haptic feedback, battery gauge reads, GPS, and the core launcher/map/message/mesh/alert flows.
  • Full e-paper first paint with readable black-on-white status and main-screen styling.
  • Built, flashed, and verified for boot, touch, and display; e-paper refresh is slower than LCD or AMOLED.
Four CI build targets: t-watch-ultra, t-watch2020-v3-s3, tdeck-plus, and tdeck-pro. Hardware-specific release filenames help prevent cross-flashing the wrong board.

What XNODE does in the field

Tactical map on device flash

  • Installs the active XTOC/XCOM raster basemap into SPIFFS.
  • Persists center, zoom, and projection metadata.
  • Scales the installed image while keeping markers aligned.
  • Supports panning, zoom controls, and recenter behavior.

Overlay sync that survives reboot

  • Stores synced markers in /spiffs/osmmap/overlays.jsonl.
  • Reloads overlays when the map opens or the watch restarts.
  • Uses transactional replacement so partial syncs do not wipe the live picture.
  • Keeps up to 96 overlay marker slots on the device.

SOS over mesh

  • Creates a clear XTOC SITREP packet.
  • Uses the device Unit ID, SOS destination, priority P1, status HELP, and current lat/lon.
  • Sends over the onboard Meshtastic service on the active channel.

Routine CheckIn

  • Creates a clear XTOC CHECKIN/LOC packet.
  • Reports Unit ID, OK status, current lat/lon, and timestamp.
  • Gives operators a fast accountability signal without opening a phone or tablet.

Operational note

XNODE is a firmware project for compact LilyGO field hardware and a companion workflow for the X Suite. The current flash-map mode is intentionally compact: one installed raster basemap with synced overlays, not a full multi-tile slippy map engine. That tradeoff keeps the workflow useful inside tight flash, RAM, battery, and screen constraints.

FAQ

Does XNODE replace XTOC or XCOM?

No. XNODE is the wearable edge. XTOC and XCOM remain the larger planning, mapping, packet, and radio workflow tools.

Does XNODE work offline?

The device can display the installed basemap and persisted overlays from flash. Syncing new maps, markers, unit settings, and pushed alerts happens through the companion XTOC/XCOM bridge.

Does the SOS button send through the phone?

No. Manual SOS builds a clear XTOC SITREP packet and hands it to the watch Meshtastic service for transmission on the active mesh channel.

Is it open source?

The active firmware source is published at github.com/MKme/xnode.
